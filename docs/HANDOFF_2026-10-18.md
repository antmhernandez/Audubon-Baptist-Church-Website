## Recommended architecture

1. **Cloudflare Pages** for the site/PWA, connected to GitHub.
2. **Cloudflare R2 Standard** for 2–4 latest sermon delivery copies (H.264/AAC MP4 + audio-only).
3. **Church Linux laptop** for masters/full historical archive and FFmpeg processing.
4. **Independent offsite encrypted backup**, not just two RAID disks or Facebook.
5. **Facebook** for live church broadcast/link and optional authorized imports; not sole archive.
6. Later, secure member accounts/database, authenticated media worker and upload path.

Avoid making church laptop the public HTTP origin for all older-video viewers. Instead keep the historical archive linked to existing church/Facebook content initially; later restore requests into R2 when needed.
