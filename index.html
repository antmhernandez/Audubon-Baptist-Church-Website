Church life should be easy to find.

What we believe

Jesus Christ is the center of our hope and the Scriptures are our final authority for faith and life.

Audubon’s current site identifies the congregation as a cooperating member of the Southern Baptist Convention and the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 as a unifying statement of belief.

  • ScriptureGod’s written Word shapes what we believe and how we live.
  • The GospelSalvation is by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ, crucified and risen.
  • The ChurchWe gather for worship, discipleship, fellowship, the ordinances, mission, and service.
Read our existing beliefs page ↗

Our story

A long history.
Renewed together.

In 2017, Audubon Baptist Church and The Church at Louisville joined together as one congregation after a season of prayer and counsel.

Audubon’s current history describes that union as a gracious work of church revitalization. The new site can honor that history without living in the past: worship, preaching, fellowship, prayer, service, and gospel mission remain the center.
