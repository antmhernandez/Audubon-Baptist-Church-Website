What we believe

Brief enough to read. Clear enough to understand.

The home page can provide a concise doctrinal introduction and link to a fuller statement approved by the church.

Scripture

We receive the Bible as God's written Word and our final authority for faith and life.

The Gospel

We proclaim salvation by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ, who died for our sins and rose again.

The Church

We are a local Baptist congregation committed to worship, discipleship, fellowship, the ordinances, mission, and service.

Southern Baptist Affiliation

Audubon Baptist Church's current site identifies the congregation as a cooperating member of the Southern Baptist Convention and the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 as a unifying statement of belief.

This brief summary reflects Audubon's current published belief page; leadership should still approve the wording used on the new production site.

Our story

A rich legacy, renewed together.

Audubon's current church history tells of a significant 2017 renewal: Audubon Baptist Church, with a long congregational legacy, and The Church at Louisville, a younger church plant, joined together as one congregation. The church describes that season as one of prayer, counsel, revitalization, and God's gracious provision.

A Church in the Park

One congregation

The new website can preserve that story while making the church's present life easier to discover: worship, preaching, fellowship, prayer, discipleship, service, and gospel mission.
