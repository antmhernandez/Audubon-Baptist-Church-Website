What we believe

Brief, clear, and anchored in Scripture.

This prototype uses concise language for review. Final wording should be approved by church leadership and linked to a fuller statement of faith.

Scripture

We receive the Bible as God's written Word and our final authority for faith and life.

The Gospel

We proclaim salvation by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ, who died for our sins and rose again.

The Church

We are a local Baptist congregation committed to worship, discipleship, fellowship, the ordinances, mission, and service.

Southern Baptist Affiliation

Audubon Baptist Church identifies with the Southern Baptist tradition and cooperative mission. Exact affiliation language should be confirmed before launch.
