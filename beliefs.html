Believing

A shared faith.
A shared confession.

Audubon’s current church-published beliefs page identifies the congregation as a cooperating member of the Southern Baptist Convention and the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 as a unifying statement of belief.

01

Confession

Audubon points to the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 as its unifying statement of belief, giving visitors and members a substantial public reference rather than a newly drafted summary.

02

Cooperation

Audubon has long described itself as cooperating with Southern Baptist churches for gospel work while remaining a local congregation with its own life, leadership, and ministry.

03

Fellowship

The church’s current beliefs page also expresses a desire to walk gladly with other churches of similar doctrine for the building up of Christ’s church.

Read further

Start with Audubon’s own published page, then read the Baptist Faith and Message in full.
