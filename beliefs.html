What we believe

Centered on Christ.
Rooted in Scripture.

Audubon’s current site identifies the congregation as a cooperating member of the Southern Baptist Convention and the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 as a unifying statement of belief.

01

Scripture

God’s written Word is our final authority for faith and life.

02

The Gospel

Salvation is by God’s grace through faith in Jesus Christ, crucified and risen.

03

The Church

We gather for worship, discipleship, fellowship, the ordinances, mission, and service.
