Our story & mission

2017

A Rich Legacy,
A Church Reborn.

Audubon’s own history uses these words to describe a long-established Louisville congregation renewed through the 2017 joining of Audubon Baptist Church and The Church at Louisville.

01

One
congregation

Audubon Baptist Church traces its founding to 1944. The Church at Louisville was planted in 2013. In 2017, after months of prayer and counsel, the two churches joined together.

Audubon’s published history presents that joining as a work of church revitalization: preserving a rich legacy while looking forward together as one congregation.

The older site closes the story with gratitude for God’s graciousness and the words, “To God be the glory.”

Pastoral leadership

Serving Audubon today.

Lead Pastor

Jeff Akin

Jeff planted The Church at Louisville in 2013 and served as its lead pastor until the church joined with Audubon. Audubon’s current pastors page identifies him as Lead Pastor.

Pastor Emeritus

Dan Hatfield, Ph.D.

Dan served as Audubon’s Senior Pastor from 2010–2020 and remains involved in church ministry. Earlier in ministry, Dan and Menda Sue served eight years as missionaries in Panama.
