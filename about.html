A long history.

01

One
congregation

In 2017, Audubon Baptist Church and The Church at Louisville joined together as one congregation after a season of prayer and counsel.

Audubon’s existing church history describes that union as a gracious work of revitalization. The aim of this new site is similar in spirit: preserve what matters, make church life easier to reach, and keep the focus on worship, Scripture, fellowship, prayer, service, and the gospel.
